# AI Usage Disclaimer

This project uses generative AI in two ways: as a **code writing tool**, and **writing assistant**. All AI-assisted content has been reviewed, edited and validated by me. I take responsibility for the final content and its accuracy, relevance and academic integrity.

## 1. Code writing tool

**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5

AI was used to help write the following code:

- **Chess960 support in LLM Chess.** AI was used to adapt LLM Chess to support Chess960 and add options for the different variations like told or not told.
- **Analysis scripts.** Writing the analysis script based on the metrics I wanted.
- **Plots.** Writing the jupyter notebook, to plot the data based on the analysis. 

The experimental design, the choice of metrics and the interpretation of the results are my own. 

## 2. Writing assistant

**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 

Used for feedback on the draft paper: checking it against the track requirements, pointing out inconsistencies, unclear passages and typos, and suggesting further analyses. I treated the suggestions as review comments, decided which ones to act on, and checked every change against my own data and figures. All text in the paper is written by me.

## 3. What was not done with AI

- Designing the experiments (start conditions, opponents, metrics) and running them.
- Interpreting the results and drawing the conclusions about reasoning vs reciting.
